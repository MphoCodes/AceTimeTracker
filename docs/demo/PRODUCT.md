# AceTimeTracker

**Track your work. Make time for what matters.**

Ace is a native Android time tracker for people who want a clear record of their work and a calmer way to focus.

## What it does

- Today: see tracked time, recent activity and category progress.
- Timer: use a stopwatch or Pomodoro focus session, with a shared running-session strip.
- Add time: record a missed session with a category, note and optional photo.
- History: search, filter and edit saved sessions, or track a category again.
- Reports: choose a period, compare it with the previous one, review category goals and share PDF or CSV.
- Local continuity: entries and categories persist locally; timer drafts recover paused. Authenticated entry/category sync retries while the app runs and on relaunch. Media uploads need a connection.
