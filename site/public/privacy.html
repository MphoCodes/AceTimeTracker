Privacy

Updated 16 November 2026. AceTimeTracker is developed by Mpho Ndlela · MphoCodes.

The download on this site is a development demo. Choose Explore the demo and use sample information. Production account services are being prepared; this page distinguishes the demo from the planned account service.

The demo on your device

Demo categories, time entries, notes, timer drafts and preferences are stored on your device. Demo entries are not synced to a server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this local data. Files you export or save elsewhere remain under your control.

When you use an account

Account-enabled builds use Google Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in, Firebase Firestore for account profiles, categories and time entries, and Firebase Storage for photos you choose to upload. Account information can include your email, name, profile photo and the details you enter about your work: categories, dates, times, time zones, notes and attached photos. These are used to provide sign-in, storage and synchronisation.

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not intentionally collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment information. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Google may process service and security information to operate Firebase. See Firebase privacy information.

Permissions and exports

Notifications show a running timer and reminders you enable. The app can restore reminder scheduling after restart. Photos are selected by you. PDF and CSV exports are created when you ask, and are shared only through the destination you choose. Other apps receiving an export have their own privacy practices.

Storage and security

Account data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections. The account database is located in Google Cloud’s South Africa region. Authentication and other service processing may occur in other countries. Access rules restrict account data to its owner. Local data remains until you remove it or clear app storage; avoid entering sensitive information into this development preview.

Deletion and retention

See Delete your account and data for the current options. The prepared account-deletion service blocks new account writes, then removes uploaded files, time entries, categories, profile data and the Firebase login. Deletion is processed in the background; an accepted request is not confirmation that every deletion has completed.

A minimal record containing the account’s internal ID, deletion status and timestamps is retained to prevent stale devices from recreating deleted data and to retry failed cleanup. It does not contain your email, name, photos or time entries. This minimal operational record is retained indefinitely to prevent delayed device writes from recreating deleted data. Previously exported files, copies saved by you and another device’s local copies are not remotely erased; clear the app’s storage on those devices. Provider security logs and backups may follow Google’s retention periods.

This website

The website is hosted by Vercel. It serves the film, screenshots and APK without advertising or analytics scripts. Vercel processes request information such as IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the service. See Vercel’s privacy policy. GitHub hosts source and release files; following a GitHub link is subject to its policies.

Contact

For support, privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email hello@mphondlela.co.za. For deletion requests, use the subject “AceTimeTracker account deletion” and contact us from the email associated with your account where possible. We may verify account ownership before deleting data. Never send your password.

The secure online deletion service is still being configured. You can contact us by email while setup continues. For public, non-sensitive feedback, you can also use the issue tracker; do not post private account details there.
