Before you try it.

A development preview for feedback. Production accounts and a Google Play release are still being prepared.

What happens to demo data?

Demo entries and categories stay on this device and persist when you close the app. They are not uploaded. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes them.

Can I use my real account?

This download uses demo authentication. Live Firebase account and sync configuration is not ready for this preview. Use sample information while trying the app.

Will the timer survive a restart?

A saved timer draft can be restored paused. Ace does not continue timing uninterrupted after force-stop or reboot.

Is this the final release?

No. This is a debug-signed development APK, not a Google Play production release. Future builds may require reinstalling. Export any sample work you want to keep before uninstalling.
