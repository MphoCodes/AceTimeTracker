Delete account & data

Remove your AceTimeTracker demo data, or request deletion of an account when account services are available.

Using the demo?

The public APK uses sample data stored locally. On Android, open Settings → Apps → AceTimeTracker → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall AceTimeTracker. This removes local entries, categories, timer drafts and preferences. Delete any PDF/CSV exports or photos you saved outside the app separately.

Account deletion

Account-enabled builds include Settings → Delete account. You must confirm the permanent deletion and re-enter your password. The service blocks further account writes and queues removal of your uploaded photos, entries, categories, profile and login.

A small operational record of the internal account ID, status and timestamps remains to prevent old devices from restoring deleted data and to retry failed cleanup. Clear app storage on other devices; exports you previously shared are not recalled. Read the privacy details.

Online deletion is not active yet because production account services are still being configured. This page will offer secure account verification before account-enabled distribution. The current downloadable demo does not create a server account.

Verify your account

Your credentials go directly to Google Firebase to verify ownership. They are not saved by this website.

To request account deletion without the app, email hello@mphondlela.co.za with the subject “AceTimeTracker account deletion”. Contact us from your account email where possible; ownership may need verification. Never send your password. The automated service is still being configured, and sending an email does not mean deletion is complete. See contact and privacy details.
