# Play artwork

The phone screenshots use disposable sample entries and were captured from the app on an isolated emulator. They should be checked against the final release build before upload.

- `app-icon.png`: the existing app logo prepared at 512 × 512 pixels.
- `feature-graphic.jpg`: the selected feature graphic exported at 1024 × 500 pixels.
- `feature-graphic-source.png`: the original generated artwork.
- `screenshots/phone/`: four 1080 × 1920 JPEG screenshots.

The feature graphic was created with the built-in image-generation tool. The app dashboard and icon were supplied as style references; the banner includes a generated interpretation of the logo. Prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium, clean Google Play feature graphic for an Android time-tracking app, with a wide landscape composition designed to work at 1024 by 500 pixels (2.048:1). The supplied app screenshot is a style reference for its existing vivid red, off-white, and deep navy palette; the supplied app icon is a brand reference only. Do not alter or redraw the icon and do not display any app screen. Use bold, clear typography with the exact headline 'AceTimeTracker' and smaller exact tagline 'Make time count'. Place an elegant abstract stopwatch and restrained progress-ring shapes as supporting illustration, using the real app palette. Keep all text and essential illustration comfortably away from the edges. No invented UI, people, device mockups, badges, rankings, statistics, additional words, or watermarks. The graphic should be flat, crisp, polished, and readable at small size.
