# Play Store listing draft

## App name

AceTimeTracker

## Short description

Track time, plan focused sessions and see your progress.

## Full description

AceTimeTracker helps you record your work and see how you spend your time.

- Run a stopwatch or use a focus timer with planned breaks.
- Organize your work into categories and set time goals.
- Log a session manually and add notes or an optional photo.
- Search and filter your timesheet history by date or category.
- Review your progress and share a timesheet report.

Whether you're studying, working on a project or keeping track of your day, AceTimeTracker gives you a clear view of the time you've spent.
